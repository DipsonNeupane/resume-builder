Paid availability

A bounded United States launch—not worldwide billing

Where new subscriptions are offered
New paid subscriptions are currently available only in the United States.
Currency
All current subscription prices are presented and charged in United States dollars (USD). No local-currency price is promised.
Payment methods
Stripe Checkout shows only payment methods enabled for the configured account and compatible with the buyer, device, USD price and subscription. APWICO does not promise a particular method before checkout displays it.
Taxes and invoices
Tax calculation, tax-inclusive pricing and invoice availability are not promised. Any tax, conversion or receipt detail must be reviewed in Stripe Checkout before payment; provider configuration requires release validation.
Refund boundary
Purchases are generally non-refundable except where mandatory consumer, unauthorized-charge, duplicate-charge or non-supply rights apply. The terms and refund policy control.

One Premium template

A monthly subscription for one selected design

The US$1.99 option unlocks only the Premium template you select in the builder. It is not a one-time template purchase and it does not include all Premium designs or Pro job-search features.

Before purchase
Previewing a template does not start checkout. A recurring paid action requires an explicit consent checkbox before Stripe checkout opens.
While active
Use that selected design for PDF and editable Word downloads under the paid reasonable-human-use policy.
After cancellation
After an individual Premium-template subscription expires, the design remains available to preview, but new downloads in that Premium design require active access. Resume content is not deleted, and files already downloaded remain yours.
If you later start Pro
When a Pro purchase succeeds, future renewals for active individual-template subscriptions are stopped. Their already-paid access continues through each paid period.

Concrete export policy

PDF keeps the design. Word keeps the content editable.

PDF
PDF preserves the selected resume design.
Word (.docx)
Word (.docx) is a clean, editable content document; it does not promise visual parity with the PDF template.
Free allowance
Free includes 3 successful PDF or Word downloads in each fixed 30-day period starting at signup. Both formats share the allowance and unused downloads do not roll over.
Paid document use
Pro uses the paid reasonable-human-use policy for every template. An individual Premium-template subscription uses it only when exporting that selected design; other designs follow their own entitlement or the Free allowance. Server-side size and abuse protections apply. No unlimited-download claim is made.
Processing boundary
Downloads require a signed-in account and explicit consent to send the current resume to APWICO for server generation. The export routes do not intentionally keep the resume or generated file as a separate permanent server copy.

Check the boundaries

No hidden purchase story

  • No plan or template promises ATS passage, interviews, job offers or hiring outcomes.
  • Stripe handles checkout; APWICO does not receive or store your full card number.
  • Active subscriptions, renewal state and cancellation controls are available in the signed-in account.
  • Your master resume stays separate from job-specific versions.
