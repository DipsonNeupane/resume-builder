Recurring: Paid subscriptions renew monthly until cancelled. Cancellation stops future renewal. Paid access continues through the current billing period.

PDF keeps the design. Word keeps the content editable.

PDF
PDF preserves the selected resume design.
Word (.docx)
Word (.docx) is a clean, editable content document; it does not promise visual parity with the PDF template.
Free allowance
Free includes 7 successful PDF or Word downloads in a rolling 7-day window. Both formats share the allowance; each successful download leaves the window seven days after it occurred.
Paid document use
Pro includes fair-use downloads for every template. Server size and abuse limits apply. Existing individual-template subscriptions retain their original access while active, but are no longer sold to new customers.
Processing boundary
Downloads require a signed-in account and explicit consent to send the current resume to APWICO for server generation. The export routes do not intentionally keep the resume or generated file as a separate permanent server copy.

Before you pay

  • No plan or template promises ATS passage, interviews, job offers, or hiring outcomes.
  • Previewing a template does not start checkout. A recurring paid action requires an explicit consent checkbox before Stripe checkout opens.
  • Stripe handles checkout; APWICO does not receive or store your full card number.
  • Active subscriptions, renewal state and cancellation controls are available in the signed-in account.
  • Your master resume stays separate from job-specific versions.
