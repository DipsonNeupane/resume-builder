PDF keeps the design. Word keeps the content editable.

PDF
PDF preserves the selected resume design.
Word (.docx)
Word (.docx) is a clean, editable content document; it does not promise visual parity with the PDF template.
Free allowance
Free includes 7 successful PDF or Word downloads in a rolling 7-day window. Both formats share the allowance; failed downloads do not count, and each counted download leaves the window 7 days after it started.
Paid document use
Pro includes fair-use downloads for every template. Server size and abuse limits apply.
Processing boundary
Downloads require a signed-in account and explicit consent to send the current resume to APWICO for server generation. The generated file is not kept. When you export a job-specific version and choose to keep it with its application, the exact resume content is stored in your account so you can retrieve what you submitted.

Limits, renewal and your data

Pro trial
Pro is free for 30 days, then US$14.99 per month. Checkout collects a payment method; cancel before the trial ends to avoid the monthly charge.
Free tailoring trial
The Free tailoring trial covers the first application you prepare with it, including up to 3 successful tailoring suggestion runs. Failed runs do not count, and the trial does not renew.
Pro limits
Pro is not unlimited: up to 10,000 tracked applications, up to 20 results per job search, fair-use downloads, and up to 20 tailoring requests per hour per account. Resume suggestions may pause when the monthly service budget is reached; other features keep working.
Renewal
Pro starts with a 30-day free trial, then renews at US$14.99 monthly until cancelled. Cancel before the trial ends to avoid the monthly charge.
Cancellation
Cancellation stops future renewal. Paid access continues through the current billing period. After Pro ends, Premium designs remain available to preview, but new downloads in a Premium design require active Pro. Resume content and application records are not deleted, and files already downloaded remain yours.
Your data
Your resumes, job-specific versions, application records and kept document copies stay in your account on Free and Pro and after Pro ends. Above the Free limit of 100 applications, every existing record stays available to open, edit, export as CSV and remove; only adding new ones waits.

Paid availability

Where new subscriptions are offered
New paid subscriptions are currently available only in the United States.
Currency
All current subscription prices are presented and charged in United States dollars (USD). No local-currency price is promised.
Payment methods
Stripe Checkout shows only payment methods enabled for the configured account and compatible with the buyer, device, USD price and subscription. APWICO does not promise a particular method before checkout displays it.
Taxes and invoices
Review any taxes, currency conversion, invoice, and receipt details in Stripe Checkout before paying. Tax-inclusive pricing and invoice availability are not promised.
Refund boundary
Purchases are generally non-refundable except where mandatory consumer, unauthorized-charge, duplicate-charge or non-supply rights apply. The terms and refund policy control.

Before you pay

  • No plan or template promises ATS passage, interviews, job offers, or hiring outcomes.
  • Previewing a template does not start checkout. A recurring paid action requires an explicit consent checkbox before Stripe checkout opens.
  • Stripe handles checkout; APWICO does not receive or store your full card number.
  • Active subscriptions, renewal state and cancellation controls are available in the signed-in account.
  • Your master resume stays separate from job-specific versions.
