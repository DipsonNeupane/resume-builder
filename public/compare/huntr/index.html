Editorial review: APWICO evidence review; reviewed 2026-09-30.

Relevant strengths

  • Huntr publishes a job-search CRM with job, contact and application tracking.
  • Its help center distinguishes base resumes from tailored resumes.
  • Its checked pricing page described free PDF export and all templates on the Free tier.

Important boundaries

  • Huntr describes resume scoring and keyword matching; APWICO uses requirement-level evidence states and leaves the pursue decision to the user.
  • The official pricing response was locale-specific and can change; users should verify the live page.
  • No claim is made about either product’s private systems or hiring outcomes.

Free and paid access

Free

The checked official pricing response described unlimited base resumes, free PDF export, all templates, two tailored resume/application packets and tracking up to 100 jobs.

Paid

The same source described recurring Pro access with unlimited tailored resumes, AI features and job tracking; verify current price, locale and cadence on the live page.

Workflow

Huntr presents base and tailored resumes inside a broader job tracker. APWICO preserves one master and creates a separate job-specific version after evidence review and user decision.

How evidence is presented

Huntr publicly describes match/scoring tools. APWICO does not present a hiring probability or ATS pass score.

Billing and cancellation

The checked official page displayed recurring monthly, quarterly and six-month options in the US/English view. Exact prices are intentionally omitted here because the current response is locale-dependent.

About APWICO

Pricing and recurring terms, product boundaries and Match behavior were reviewed 2026-09-30 under the same evidence standard.

  • The public interface reviewed here is English-only; Unicode and right-to-left document support are not full interface localization.
  • The browser extension is unpublished, so APWICO makes no public extension-availability claim.
  • No testimonial, hiring outcome, ATS acceptance or interview-rate claim is established.
  • Account cloud saving and paid access remain subject to the published privacy, terms and entitlement boundaries.
