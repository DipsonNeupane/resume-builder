Reviewed September 30, 2026.

Relevant strengths

  • Teal publishes an integrated job tracker and resume workflow.
  • Its official pricing page describes unlimited resumes on Free and Teal+ and multiple subscription durations.
  • Its help center documents builder, matcher, import, analysis and export workflows.

Important boundaries

  • Teal describes keyword matching and a Match Score; APWICO centers requirement-level source wording and evidence states without a dominant percentage.
  • Vendor pages cannot establish private processing behavior or applicant outcomes.
  • Pricing and plan limits can change and must be rechecked after the displayed review date.

Free and paid access

Free

The official US/English pricing page displayed a Free plan with unlimited resumes and job tracking, 10 resume templates and a limited top-five keyword view.

Paid

The same page displayed Teal+ weekly, 30-day and 90-day recurring options with full keyword analysis and additional paid features.

Workflow

Teal combines resume creation, matching and job tracking. APWICO combines a master resume, requirement-level evidence review, a user pursue decision and separate job-specific versions.

How evidence is presented

Teal publicly describes keywords and Match Score. APWICO shows the requirement, employer source wording and resume evidence together, including “not demonstrated” as an unknown rather than a hiring verdict.

Billing and cancellation

Teal’s checked pricing/help sources describe recurring access and cancellation at the end of the billing period. Confirm the current displayed terms at purchase.
