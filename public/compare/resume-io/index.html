This page compares current, vendor-described public facts with APWICO’s published boundaries. Verify the purchase screen before paying.

Editorial review: APWICO evidence review; reviewed 2026-09-30.

Relevant strengths

  • Resume.io publishes a broad builder and template workflow.
  • Its help center documents PDF and, for some templates, Word export.
  • Its billing and cancellation help articles state what happens after cancellation.

Important boundaries

  • Resume.io says prices vary by location, so this page does not invent one global price.
  • Its free export is limited to a named template or text format according to the checked help source.
  • No private data-flow, ATS-success or hiring-outcome claim is inferred.

Free and paid access

Free

The checked help page describes free resume building and free download using the Vancouver template or plain TXT.

Paid

Premium documentation describes unlimited resumes, cover letters and PDF downloads, with Word export available for some templates.

Workflow

Resume.io focuses on document creation and templates. APWICO links the master document to job evidence, a user decision and a separate tailored version.

How evidence is presented

This comparison did not establish a public requirement-level evidence workflow in Resume.io. That means “not established,” not that no feature exists.

Billing and cancellation

Resume.io’s help center says the seven-day trial auto-renews, location affects price, and cancellation retains premium access until the end of the paid period; documents remain available afterward.

APWICO facts and boundaries

Pricing and recurring terms, product boundaries and Match behavior were reviewed 2026-09-30 under the same evidence standard.

  • The public interface reviewed here is English-only; Unicode and right-to-left document support are not full interface localization.
  • The browser extension is unpublished, so APWICO makes no public extension-availability claim.
  • No testimonial, hiring outcome, ATS acceptance or interview-rate claim is established.
  • Account cloud saving and paid access remain subject to the published privacy, terms and entitlement boundaries.
