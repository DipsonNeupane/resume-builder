Editorial review: APWICO evidence review; reviewed 2026-09-30.

Relevant strengths

  • Jobscan publishes a resume matcher that explains keyword comparison.
  • It publishes a free job tracker with application, schedule and contact organization.
  • Its public pages connect matching with job-search organization.

Important boundaries

  • Jobscan describes match rate and keyword analysis; APWICO centers inspectable requirement-to-evidence rows.
  • No current numerical price is published here because a stable same-session official pricing record was not established.
  • Public pages do not prove private processing behavior or applicant outcomes.

Free and paid access

Free

Jobscan’s public matcher and job-tracker pages describe free entry points; exact usage limits must be verified on the current product surface.

Paid

Current paid price and entitlement details are not established in this comparison. Follow Jobscan’s current purchase surface before deciding.

Workflow

Jobscan connects matching and job tracking. APWICO adds an explicit pursue/skip/unsure decision and creates a separate job-specific resume without changing the master.

How evidence is presented

Jobscan publicly frames analysis through match rate and keyword breakdown. APWICO uses demonstrated, worth reviewing and not demonstrated states tied to source wording.

Billing and cancellation

Not established from a stable official pricing source during the 2026-09-30 review; no cadence or cancellation claim is inferred.

  • No testimonial, hiring outcome, ATS acceptance or interview-rate claim is established.
  • Account cloud saving and paid access remain subject to the published privacy, terms and entitlement boundaries.
