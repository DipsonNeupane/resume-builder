General and operations manager resume evidence guide

Make leadership scope, operational decisions, resource stewardship and team outcomes visible without relying on seniority language alone.

Occupation research is a question set—not proof.

Represents leadership across industries in US and Australian classifications; it also tests whether the evidence model can represent scope without inflating authority.

Sources reviewed September 30, 2026; next review December 29, 2026. This is not professional or regulatory approval.

What the named jurisdiction sources describe

O*NET describes planning or directing operations, policy, resources and performance. The title spans very different organization sizes and industries.

Structure the resume around evidence

  • State the organization, function and scope needed to understand the role.
  • Separate operational, people, financial and strategic evidence.
  • Show decision authority and governance honestly.
  • Prefer specific outcomes and operating changes over leadership adjectives.

Flexible sections

  • Leadership scope
  • Selected transformations
  • Operations and governance
  • Board or stakeholder work
  • Industry credentials
  • Community leadership

Skills belong beside context

  • Tie resource planning to an operating decision.
  • Describe people leadership through team scope and your actual responsibility.
  • Connect policy or process changes to the problem and observable effect.

Common requirement patterns to verify

  • Direct a function or operating unit
  • Allocate people, budget or other resources
  • Set or implement policy and operating plans
  • Monitor performance and resolve cross-functional issues

Make one bullet inspectable

Weak, fictional example: Strategic leader responsible for operations and high-performing teams.

Stronger, fictional example: Led scheduling and service operations for a five-site program, establishing a weekly capacity review and clarifying escalation ownership across site leads.

Map requirements to evidence

Example requirement: Multi-site operations leadership with budget accountability

Direct evidenceSite scope, team or function authority and budget responsibility are all documented.
Worth reviewingYou coordinated work across sites but did not hold the budget; that may transfer but should not be relabelled.
Not demonstrated“Operations leadership” appears without scale, authority or a decision example. This is an unknown in the document, not proof the person lacks the qualification.

Suggested template

Boardroom: Boardroom groups multiple roles at one employer to show leadership progression.

Regional boundary

The same title can mean different scope by employer, sector and country. Describe the operating reality instead of relying on title equivalence.
