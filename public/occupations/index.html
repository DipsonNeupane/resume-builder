Resume evidence by occupation

A job title is a starting point. The actual posting, local requirements and your real evidence still control the application.

Use occupation data as a question set

Occupation sources show common tasks and work context. Use them to identify evidence from your experience, then verify everything against the actual job. Do not copy generic duties into your resume.

Check local requirements

The International Labour Organization’s ISCO-08 organizes jobs by their tasks and duties for international reporting. National systems add local detail. Any licence, education, wage, or outlook fact remains attached to its jurisdiction and source date.

What each guide includes

  • A dated, jurisdiction-labelled source record
  • Central tasks translated into questions, not copied resume claims
  • Examples of direct, adjacent and not-demonstrated evidence
  • Regulated or employer-specific requirements kept distinct
  • A route into the real job-description evidence check
