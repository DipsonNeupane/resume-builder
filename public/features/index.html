How ResumeStride works

Decide where your effort belongs.

Start with the role, compare its requirements with evidence you actually have, make your own pursuit decision, then tailor a separate version without changing the master resume.

The decision path

Five questions, in the order they become useful.

  1. Am I qualified for this job?
  2. Does my experience demonstrate what they want?
  3. Is this job worth applying to?
  4. What is missing or needs clarification?
  5. How should I tailor for this specific job?

Product boundaries

No score theater. No invented experience. No silent master edits.

ResumeStride shows what the reviewed documents demonstrate and what remains unknown. The user keeps decision authority, and every job-specific resume stays separate from the master.
