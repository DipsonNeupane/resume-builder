Tailor without overwriting

Job-specific resume versions

Focus a resume on one role while preserving the master as the stable source of truth.

01

One version, one saved job

A job-specific resume is created explicitly from an eligible saved job and records which master revision it came from.

02

Review every suggestion

Grounded suggestions can be accepted or rejected individually. Rejected suggestions do not change the document.

03

Master changes stay non-destructive

When the master changes, APWICO offers a keep-or-reset choice instead of silently merging or overwriting the job-specific version.

Important boundaries

What this capability does not promise

  • Suggestions must be supported by the resume; they cannot add unprovided experience or outcomes.
  • Starting and using a persistent job-specific resume requires the applicable account access shown in the product.
  • APWICO does not submit the application or infer that you applied.
