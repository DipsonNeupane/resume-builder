Evidence before effort

Requirement-level Match

See each job requirement beside evidence from your resume.

01

Source wording stays visible

Match keeps each extracted requirement connected to the wording in the job description so you can review the interpretation.

02

Evidence stays connected

Direct evidence, adjacent evidence worth reviewing and not-demonstrated items are shown at requirement level instead of collapsed into one score.

03

The decision stays yours

You decide whether a role is worth pursuing. Match does not issue an apply verdict, hiring prediction or dominant ATS percentage.

Limits

  • “No evidence found in this resume” means the reviewed resume did not show evidence; it does not mean you lack the qualification.
  • Job descriptions can be incomplete or ambiguous, so extracted requirements require review.
  • The free check reviews up to 12 requirements. Saved Match lets you review more requirements and keep your clarifications.
