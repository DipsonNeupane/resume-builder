Master resume builder

Keep your full experience in one master resume. Tailor separate copies without changing it.

01

Import or start fresh

Start with a Word document, a text-based PDF or a blank resume. Imports are parsed locally in the browser and must be reviewed before the resume becomes usable.

02

Flexible experience

Sections can describe employment, education, projects, volunteering, certifications, research and other relevant experience across occupations and countries.

03

Separate versions

A job-specific resume is a separate version linked to one saved job. Editing it does not silently change the master resume.

Limits

  • Imported PDF structure is best-effort; scanned, encrypted or unreadable PDFs are rejected instead of guessed.
  • Browser drafts are local unless the product explicitly shows that cloud saving is enabled.
  • APWICO does not invent experience, credentials, tools or results.
