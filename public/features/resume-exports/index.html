Concrete format policy

PDF and editable Word exports

Choose the format for the work it needs to do: a PDF that preserves the selected design or an editable Word document that prioritizes content.

01

PDF preserves the design

Server-generated PDF uses the selected APWICO template, content direction and A4 or Letter page size.

02

Word stays editable

The Word export is a clean editable document. It preserves content rather than promising visual parity with the PDF template.

03

The current document is exported

Export controls apply to the resume currently being edited—master or job-specific—without silently changing the other document.

Important boundaries

What this capability does not promise

  • Sign-in and explicit upload consent are required before resume content is sent for document generation.
  • Free accounts share three successful PDF or Word downloads per fixed 30-day period from signup.
  • Paid downloads are subject to the documented reasonable-human-use policy; access and recurring terms are shown before checkout.
