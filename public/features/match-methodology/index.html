Match methodology

How Match compares a job and resume

Match compares a job description with your resume. It supports your decision; it does not predict hiring, assign an ATS score, or tell you to apply.

01

Requirements come from the posting

APWICO looks for explicit qualifications in the job description, keeps each item linked to its source wording, and groups it as required, preferred, or nice to have.

02

Direct evidence stays traceable

Evidence found means wording in the reviewed resume directly supports the requirement. The product shows the connected resume wording so the user can check whether the connection is meaningful.

03

Related evidence is not a pass

Related evidence — review means the resume contains related wording, but the evidence may be incomplete or indirect. It is presented for review, not upgraded into direct evidence automatically.

04

No evidence found remains unknown

No evidence found in this resume means the reviewed resume did not show supporting evidence. It does not mean the person lacks the skill, credential or experience, and it does not become a rejection recommendation.

05

Confirmed incompatibility requires the user

A required item becomes a confirmed incompatibility only when the user explicitly says they do not have it. Preferred or nice-to-have items remain non-demonstrated rather than becoming disqualifiers.

06

Clarifications change the evidence view

The user can confirm that they can demonstrate an unresolved requirement, state that they do not have it or remain unsure. Saved Match Analysis is recalculated with that explicit clarification and keeps the decision with the user.

07

Match and AI are separate

The current Match classification is deterministic and does not use a generative-AI model. AI is used only for separately requested tailoring suggestions; those suggestions must stay grounded in supplied resume content and are never applied without review.

Limits

  • Extraction can miss, split or misclassify ambiguous job wording; the source text remains visible so the user can correct their interpretation.
  • Wording overlap is not proof of competence, recency, proficiency or employer acceptance. Human context still matters.
  • The public browser-only evidence check uses a smaller review scope than the saved full analysis and cannot see experience omitted from the pasted resume.
  • Job descriptions may be incomplete, unlawful, outdated or inaccurate. APWICO does not verify the employer or replace professional, legal or immigration advice.
