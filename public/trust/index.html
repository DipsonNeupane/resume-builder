Product facts, not borrowed credibility

How APWICO handles your data and decisions

This page records implemented boundaries. It does not use seals, testimonials, hiring promises or ATS claims to make the product look more proven than it is.

Factual trust record

Product and data boundaries

Your decision stays yours.

Match connects each job requirement to source wording and resume evidence. “Not demonstrated” means the resume did not show evidence; it does not claim you lack the qualification. APWICO does not decide whether you should apply.

Your master stays separate.

Tailoring starts a separate job-specific version. Accepting, rejecting or editing a suggestion does not rewrite the master resume.

Preview is separate from purchase.

Every template can be previewed. Checkout starts only after a paid option is selected and recurring consent is checked.

Export formats have different jobs.

PDF preserves the selected resume design. Word (.docx) is a clean, editable content document; it does not promise visual parity with the PDF template.

Imports stay in the browser.

Word and text-based PDF files selected through the builder are parsed locally. Server generation and optional AI processing occur only when the user requests those separate features.

Public claims have limits.

No plan or template promises ATS passage, interviews, job offers or hiring outcomes. Extension and provider capabilities are described only where the corresponding behavior is released and verified.

Customer evidence

Customer evidence will stay specific and consented.

APWICO does not publish customer stories until each quote or case study has explicit, scoped consent and factual review. A customer can ask support to withdraw future public display. Collection must exclude resume text, job text and contact details that are not necessary for the agreed story. We do not substitute anonymous counters, invented names or outcome claims.
