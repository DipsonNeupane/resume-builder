What the extractor does

It separates required, preferred, and other signals. Each item includes the employer’s source wording so you can check it.

What it does not do

It does not decide whether you should apply or predict hiring outcomes. Ambiguous wording stays under “Other signals.”

How to use the result

Review required items first, then decide what your resume clearly demonstrates, what is worth reviewing and what remains not demonstrated. Preferred items can matter, but they should not automatically be treated as application blockers.

Limits

Extraction can miss or misclassify unclear wording, and a posting may omit criteria used later. Check every result against the source.
