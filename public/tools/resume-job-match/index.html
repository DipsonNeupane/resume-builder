What this comparison does

It compares up to 12 job requirements with supporting wording in your resume and shows both sources together.

How to read the labels

  • Evidence found: the resume contains direct, substantial wording related to the requirement.
  • Related evidence — review: related wording exists, but the evidence may be incomplete or indirect.
  • No evidence found in this resume: this limited check did not find support. That is an unknown—not proof you lack the qualification.

What it does not do

This is not an ATS score, candidate score, hiring probability or interview forecast. It does not infer experience that is absent from the text, and it does not label silence as a confirmed incompatibility.

Why the free check is limited

The free check may miss equivalent skills or context. Saved Match keeps your resume, job, and clarifications together for a more detailed review.
