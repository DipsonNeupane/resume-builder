Template structure

Structure
Academic CV conventions: date rail for education and appointments, research-first order, publications numbered and formatted as citations (quoted title, italic venue, year) with hanging numbers. Multi-page is expected.
Section order
Research interests → Education → Appointments → Publications → Grants & awards → Teaching → Presentations → Service → Skills
Typography
Newsreader throughout; small-caps section headings; no sans.

Features

  • Single Column
  • Research First
  • Numbered Publications
  • Date Rail
  • Serif
  • Multi Page Friendly
