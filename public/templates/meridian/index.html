How this design is organized

Structure
Hanging headings: section labels sit in a start-side rail beside their content; the header mirrors the rail with contact details stacked in it. Body text stays a single reading column.
Section order
Profile → Skills → sections in user order
Typography
Instrument Sans throughout; hierarchy by weight and size contrast.
Distinct from other designs
Every free template stacks headings above content; Meridian’s rail turns section labels into a scannable index down the page.
