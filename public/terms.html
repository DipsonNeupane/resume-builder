Free plan

A Free account has no subscription and no charge. It includes the limits below; the app shows what remains before you act.

Downloads. When generated downloads are available, a signed-in Free account includes seven (7) successful PDF or Word document downloads in any rolling seven-day period. PDF and Word share that allowance. A download counts from the time it starts and stops counting seven days later; a failed download does not count, and unused downloads do not accumulate. A safe retry of the same document request does not use another download, although a new, changed, or different-format request may. The app shows availability and errors before returning a file.

Tracked applications. A Free account can track up to one hundred (100) applications, counting every saved job and every application you add, paste or import, whatever its stage. At the limit you can still open, edit, change the stage of, export and remove every existing application; only adding a new one is refused until you remove one or upgrade. APWICO never hides or deletes an application because of this limit.

Tailoring trial. Each Free account includes one (1) complete tailoring trial. The trial becomes tied to the first application you prepare with it and then covers that application only, including up to three (3) successful AI suggestion runs for it. A run that fails does not count. The trial does not renew, cannot be moved to another application and has no cash value.

Job search. Free includes three (3) manual job-search refreshes in any rolling twenty-four-hour period, with up to ten (10) relevant results per refresh, and an optional automatic refresh about once a day. Results depend on an external job provider and may be incomplete.

Pro subscription

When paid checkout is enabled, Pro costs US$14.99 per month and is offered to new subscribers only in the United States. It includes all 27 templates, full requirement-level Match Analysis, job-specific resumes and AI tailoring suggestions for every saved application, and the other Pro features listed at checkout. Pro renews automatically each month until cancelled. The purchase screen shows the recurring amount and requires your express consent before checkout. Stripe Checkout displays the final amount and any provider-presented tax or conversion detail before payment; tax-inclusive pricing and invoice availability are not promised.

Pro limits. Pro is not unlimited. It includes up to 10,000 tracked applications, up to twenty (20) results per job search, fair-use document downloads subject to server size and abuse protections, and up to twenty (20) AI tailoring requests per hour per account. AI suggestions may also pause for everyone when the service’s monthly AI spending limit is reached; the rest of the service keeps working.

Cancellation and access

You may cancel at any time through the billing controls shown in your account. Cancellation stops future renewal; Pro access continues through the billing period already paid for, and the account then returns to Free. After Pro ends, Premium designs remain available to preview but new downloads in them require active Pro; you can choose a Free design and use the Free download allowance instead. If you track more than one hundred applications when Pro ends, every one of them stays available to open, edit, export and remove; adding new ones requires being under the Free limit. Files already downloaded remain yours. Ending Pro never deletes your resumes, job-specific versions, application records or kept document copies. We keep durable records of required billing notices. Your cancellation rights do not depend on receiving any email from us.

Your data and access

Your content stays available to you on Free and on Pro, and after Pro ends. You can export your applications as a CSV file and download your resumes in the formats your plan allows. To request access, correction or deletion of your account data, contact support as described in the privacy notice.
