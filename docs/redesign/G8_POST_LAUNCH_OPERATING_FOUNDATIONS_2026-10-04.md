# G8 post-launch operating foundations — Tasks 58–61

Prepared 2026-10-04. These materials create repeatable, privacy-safe operating
systems. They do not claim participants, partnerships, distribution, samples,
or findings that do not exist.

## 58 — Representative advisory group

### Charter

Review whether APWICO helps people interpret real job requirements, find truthful
evidence in their experience, decide where to spend effort, and tailor without
overwriting the master resume. The group advises; it does not endorse APWICO,
approve marketing claims, or make product decisions.

### Recruitment matrix

Recruit 10–14 people across at least six regions and all of these evidence shapes:
technical, healthcare, operations, education, service, skilled trades, research,
creative, and leadership. Include career changers, first-time applicants,
returners, people using assistive technology, people using mobile-first/low-power
devices, and both LTR and RTL language experience. No occupation, country, or
customer segment may exceed one third of the group.

Screener records only region band, occupation family, career-stage band,
accessibility/device needs, preferred language, and availability. Do not collect
resume or job text in the recruiting tracker. Compensation, contracts, consent,
and outreach are owner-approved before contact.

### Review cycle and rubric

Quarterly 75-minute sessions; one onboarding session; asynchronous review only by
explicit opt-in. Each session uses fictional or participant-redacted material.
Score: comprehension of evidence states; visibility of user authority; master vs
job-specific separation; storage state; regional/occupation assumptions;
accessibility; and claims that felt stronger than the evidence. Publish an
anonymized decision log, not participant quotations without separate permission.

### Outreach draft

> APWICO is forming a small paid advisory group of job seekers and career-support
> practitioners across occupations and regions. The group will review whether an
> evidence-first job workflow is understandable, truthful, accessible, and useful.
> Participation is not an endorsement, and no resume or job content is required
> for the initial conversation.

**Current status:** operating preparation complete; recruitment, compensation,
consent, and real participation are external/owner gates.

## 59 — Partnerships

### Evidence-backed target types

Priority is based on publicly documented job-search/career-support activity, not
an inference that any organization wants a relationship:

1. Public workforce and career services that already help people interpret role
   requirements and prepare applications.
2. University/community-college career centers serving first jobs and transitions.
3. Nonprofits supporting displaced workers, immigrants/refugees, disability
   employment, caregivers returning to work, or sector transitions.
4. Professional and trade associations with verified career-support programs.

Landscape references checked 2026-10-04:

- UK National Careers Service publishes career-decision and CV guidance:
  https://nationalcareers.service.gov.uk/careers-advice/careers-advice
- Canada Job Bank provides career planning, employment-centre links, job search,
  and resume resources:
  https://www.jobbank.gc.ca/career-planning/resources
- Workforce Singapore documents Careers Connect/coaching services:
  https://www.wsg.gov.sg/home/individuals/career-services.html
- U.S. CareerOneStop connects job seekers with American Job Centers:
  https://www.careeronestop.org/LocalHelp/AmericanJobCenters/american-job-centers.aspx

These are examples for target research, not claimed partners.

### Qualification and pilot

Score each target on audience fit, geographic/occupation contribution, existing
human support, accessibility, data governance, procurement burden, and ability to
run a bounded pilot. Reject pay-to-endorse, bulk resume ingestion, employer
screening, or any arrangement asking APWICO to rank people.

A pilot offers staff orientation, a fictional demonstration, participant opt-in,
direct participant accounts, no partner access to documents, and aggregate
reporting only when a disclosure-safe minimum is met. Success measures are task
completion, clarity, time bands, voluntary outcomes, and support burden—not lead
volume alone.

### Outreach draft and tracker

> Your program already helps people make career and application decisions.
> APWICO is testing a bounded evidence-first workflow that lets a participant
> compare one role with their own resume, see what is demonstrated or unclear,
> and decide whether to tailor a separate version. We are seeking a small pilot
> with explicit participant consent and no partner access to resume/job content.

Tracker fields: target, public source, source checked date, audience, geography,
contact channel, qualification score, owner, stage, last action, next action,
pilot scope, privacy review, contract status. Do not store scraped personal data.

**Current status:** target model, evidence, outreach, due diligence, and pilot
structure complete; outreach, agreements, and real partnerships are owner gates.

## 60 — Organizational distribution

### Bounded pilot model

- 4–8 weeks; 20–100 invited participants; voluntary individual accounts.
- Organization receives a neutral invite link and facilitator guide, not access
  to user resumes, jobs, Match results, decisions, outcomes, or accounts.
- APWICO remains a decision-support tool; it is not used for admission,
  employment screening, compliance, or worker evaluation.
- Organization-specific campaign code is content-free and optional. Aggregate
  reporting requires at least 20 eligible participants and suppresses any cell
  below 10; no occupation/region breakdown if it risks re-identification.
- Support, accessibility, deletion, incident, opt-out, and pilot-end procedures
  are named before launch. No automatic renewal or conversion into a paid plan.

Readiness packet: facilitator one-pager, fictional walkthrough, participant
consent text, privacy/data-flow diagram, accessibility statement, support path,
incident contact, success rubric, weekly issue log, and closeout template.

Product infrastructure is already sufficient for direct participant journeys,
content-free campaign attribution, consented outcome reporting, and owner-scoped
accounts. A partner/admin dashboard is intentionally not built without validated
need because it would expand privacy and authorization risk.

**Current status:** distribution and privacy model complete; institution choice,
contract, participants, and any custom reporting are owner/external gates.

## 61 — Original research

### Research question and preregistration

Primary question: among consenting APWICO users, what share reach a genuine
requirement-level evidence review and later prepare a job-specific application
package? Secondary descriptive questions cover decision clarity, self-reported
time saved, and voluntarily reported application outcomes.

Before analysis, freeze: population, date window, inclusion/exclusion rules,
metric version, treatment of repeat events, missingness rules, subgroup policy,
minimum sample, and planned tables. Timestamp the plan in the repository. Changes
after inspection are labelled exploratory.

### Privacy-safe data requirements

Use only durable content-free conversion/review records, closed-vocabulary funnel
events, consented bounded outcome reports, plan/scope/time buckets, and aggregate
performance. Never export resume text, job text/URL, names, email, notes, contacts,
AI prompts/responses, payment identifiers, or raw searches into research data.
Owner IDs are used only for deduplication inside the controlled system and are
replaced with study-specific one-way keys before analysis.

### Minimums and reporting

- No overall conversion estimate below 100 eligible owners or 30 conversions.
- No subgroup estimate below 50 eligible owners and 20 outcomes; suppress cells
  below 10. Predeclare wider minimums for rare or sensitive groups.
- Report counts, denominator definition, window, missingness, confidence interval,
  methodology version, and collection mode.
- Separate platform-observed actions from self-reports. Do not infer causality,
  employer behavior, qualification, or representativeness from either.
- Interview/offer rates require explicit self-report and must say APWICO did not
  verify or cause the result. No testimonial is derived from outcome data.
- Publish null/low-sample results and corrections. Archive the exact query/version
  and disclosure review with every public artifact.

Analysis proceeds only after privacy review and the minimum sample. External peer
or practitioner review is sought before public claims; conflicts and limitations
are disclosed.

**Current status:** methodology and publication standards complete; sufficient
samples, analysis, independent review, and findings are legitimately post-launch.
