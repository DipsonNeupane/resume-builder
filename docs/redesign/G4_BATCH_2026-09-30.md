## Source-of-truth architecture

`src/content/commercial.ts` is the public commercial contract. It contains the three plan paths, recurring/cancellation/export boundaries and the empty consent-gated proof registry. `scripts/render-templates.ts` renders the static pricing and trust pages from that contract. Checkout and entitlement remain server-authoritative; static content cannot grant access or start a purchase.

The repository verifies the published facts against implemented billing behavior:

- offer catalog: US$1.99/month for one selected Premium template and US$19.99/month for Pro, USD, monthly;
- US-only new-subscription check;
- explicit `renewalConsent: true` server requirement and unchecked client consent;
- end-of-period cancellation and entitlement preservation;
- Pro activation stops future individual-template renewals after successful payment;
- Free shared PDF/Word allowance and paid reasonable-human-use server protections;
- PDF template rendering versus clean editable DOCX without visual-parity promise.

## Customer proof boundary

There are no published testimonials, case studies, star ratings, counters, seals or guarantees. A future proof record cannot render unless it has a bounded identifier and copy, scoped public consent, factual-review/moderation/publication dates, no URL/email embedded in public story text, and `support@resumestride.com` as the withdrawal route. Resume text, job text and unnecessary contact data are outside this registry.
