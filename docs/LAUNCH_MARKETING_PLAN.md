# ResumeStride first-month marketing plan

Drafted September 19, 2026. Approved scope: planning only. No publishing, messages, purchases, ad accounts or campaigns executed. Existing engineering work continues separately.

## Positioning

Working proposition: “Your experience, clearly presented. Build a resume free; get job-specific help when you need it.”

Public beta message today: “Build and print a clean resume free. Three templates, editable sections and a draft saved on your device.” Use only verified live features. Account storage, dedicated PDF allowances, Pro tailoring and the extension must not be advertised as available until released and verified.

Future paid message, after launch gates pass: “Tailor your real experience to a job with a $19.99, 30-day Pro pass. Review every change. Recurring payment is optional.” Show actual generation limits before checkout. Three free generated PDFs per 30 days from signup is the approved planned allowance; browser printing and JSON backups are separate. Do not promise unlimited AI or downloads inconsistent with entitlements.

Avoid “guaranteed interviews,” “beats every ATS,” “first ever extension,” “works on every website,” or an unverified traffic comparison. No claim to detect whether personal history is true. Paid help is convenience and relevant presentation, not a promise that customers will be selected.

## Who to reach first

Keep the product open to worldwide users and every occupation. Focus the initial marketing messages, not eligibility, on people who have useful experience but struggle to describe it: first-job applicants, people returning to work, career changers and workers in service/trades roles. These are proposed campaign angles, not validated claims that competitors ignore these users. Start English-language materials because that is the current UI; multilingual resume content is different from translated UI or localized advice.

## Priority channels

1. Demonstration content: prepare three short, captioned videos using fictional data: build a first resume; describe a retail/service responsibility clearly; recover a draft with a JSON backup. Show the real exported result. Post only after separate authorization. Future tailoring demos must wait for working generation; no staged fake AI responses.
2. Career-support organizations: research 10 potential contacts across libraries, training providers, community colleges and employment-support groups. American Job Centers already provide resume help, making them a possible referral audience, not an assumed partnership. Start locally for practical outreach, while leaving global access open. Prepare tailored drafts; send only with explicit authorization. Never claim affiliation or endorsement.
3. Helpful community participation: answer concrete resume questions in communities whose rules permit participation. Disclose affiliation when relevant, request moderator permission where required, and avoid bulk posting or unsolicited direct messages. Reddit has platform-wide anti-spam rules plus community-specific rules; there is no universal safe promotion ratio.
4. Search content: prepare three substantive guides with fictional before/after examples: first resume with no paid experience; turning everyday responsibilities into accurate resume bullets; checking a PDF before applying. Link to the actual free builder. Search visibility is a longer-term channel, not a first-month traffic promise. Avoid mass-produced occupation pages or country advice without reliable review.

No paid advertising in this plan. The $25/month approval is for AI usage, not marketing. Any paid creator deal or ad campaign needs a separate amount/channel approval. Dates are relative to marketing kickoff; engineering readiness can move paid announcements later without stopping honest beta content.

## Measurement and economics

Suggested funnel: attributable landing visit → builder start → meaningful draft completion → confirmed generated PDF success (when available) → Pro purchase (when available). For current browser printing, record only print-dialog invocation if analytics is implemented; do not call it a completed download. Track 7-day return usage, support requests and failed exports alongside acquisition. No fabricated baseline or promised customer count; first week establishes one.

Use channel-level UTM tags without names/emails/resume content. Analytics is a proposal, not installed by this document. Do not record resume text, job descriptions or session replay. Any collection must match the privacy notice and applicable consent requirements. Avoid sending drafts to advertising platforms.

Contribution per pass = $19.99 minus actual payment fees, AI generation cost, export/storage cost, refunds/chargebacks and support cost. Calculate from real invoices/usage before choosing an acquisition budget. Track activation/conversion and contribution together; cheap clicks can still be expensive customers. Do not invent lifetime value or assume subscriptions renew.
