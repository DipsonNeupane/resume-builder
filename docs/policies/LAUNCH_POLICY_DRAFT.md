Prepared for owner review. This is a product-specific draft, not a claim of universal legal compliance. ## Proposed terms text

ResumeStride helps you create resumes and prepare application materials. You remain responsible for checking accuracy before sharing any document. We do not guarantee interviews, employment, or acceptance by every applicant tracking system. Do not include information you are not entitled to use, impersonate another person, or abuse the service through automated requests.

You retain your rights to your resume and application content. You allow ResumeStride and its service providers to process it only as needed to provide the features you choose. AI suggestions must be reviewed; they can be inaccurate and must not be used to invent qualifications or experience.

Free accounts receive three app-generated resume PDF downloads in each 30-day period measured from signup. Ordinary browser printing and JSON backups do not use this allowance. Paid Pro access costs USD 19.99 for 30 days. Manual purchases do not renew automatically. A purchase made during existing paid access adds 30 days after the current paid-through time. Expired users can buy a new pass. Existing content is not deleted merely because Pro expires.

Automatic renewal is optional, off by default, and must be explicitly enabled. Its price, next charge date and cancellation controls must be shown before consent. Turning renewal off stops future renewal charges while preserving time already purchased. These statements must be backed by working billing implementation before publication.

Contact support@resumestride.com for support and billing questions. Statutory consumer rights are not waived by these terms.

## Proposed refund approach — owner decision needed

Recommendation for an approachable launch: offer a simple refund request window and refunds for duplicate charges or a material failure to deliver purchased features. The duration and treatment of heavily used passes/partial refunds must be selected by the operator, implemented in Stripe/entitlements, and reviewed for applicable mandatory rights. No blanket “no refunds” or invented 14-day universal legal rule.

## Privacy text to finalize against implementation

Account: Supabase processes your email and session information for authentication. Local drafts stay in this browser unless you explicitly save/import them to your account. Once cloud storage is enabled, account resume data is stored through Supabase under account ownership controls.

Content: resumes may contain contact details, employment and education history and anything you choose to enter. Only collect fields needed for user-selected features. Do not request government identifiers or unnecessary sensitive information. Do not log resume fields, prompts, letters or job descriptions in analytics.

Payments: Stripe will process payment information when billing is enabled. ResumeStride should store only the payment identifiers/status needed for receipts and access, not full payment-card details.

AI: before AI is enabled, name the actual provider and describe the content sent for the selected feature. Do not claim zero retention or no training without verifying the selected account/provider terms and configuration. No live AI integration exists yet.

Infrastructure: Vercel is the selected host; Google Fonts currently receives font requests from the browser. Accurately disclose external requests or self-host the permitted font assets.

Controls: users can export local JSON backups. Account export/deletion and cloud retention are not yet implemented. Do not publish a claim that they are. Set a concrete deletion/backup retention procedure before launch; financial records may require separate retention.
