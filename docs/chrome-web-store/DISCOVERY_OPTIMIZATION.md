### Full description

Save one supported job posting to ResumeStride, then compare its requirements with
the evidence in your resume. When a role is worth pursuing, create a separate
job-specific resume while your master resume stays unchanged.

HOW IT WORKS

- Open a supported public job page and click ResumeStride Job Capture.
- Review and edit the captured title, company, location, source, and description.
- Send the reviewed details to ResumeStride, sign in, and choose “Save job and see
  how I match.”
- Review what your resume clearly demonstrates, partly demonstrates, or does not yet
  demonstrate for that role.
- If you continue, create a separate job-specific resume and review each optional AI
  suggestion before accepting or rejecting it.

WHY RESUMESTRIDE IS DIFFERENT

- Your master resume remains unchanged.
- Match is evidence-based and works through the role's requirements instead of
  presenting a hiring probability.
- “Not demonstrated” means the evidence is not currently in the resume; it does not
  claim that you lack the qualification.
- Each role can have its own separate resume version.
- AI suggestions stay under your control. Nothing is accepted automatically.

SUPPORTED PUBLIC JOB PAGES

- Greenhouse public job pages.
- Selected Lever pages whose job URL contains a stable UUID.
- Pages with one reliable, unambiguous JSON-LD `JobPosting`, including some Workday
  pages that expose that metadata.

ResumeStride does not claim universal job-site support. LinkedIn and Indeed are not
supported. JSON-LD support means the page provides one structured job posting with
the required title, employer, description, and reliable page identity; it does not
mean “works everywhere.” If capture is unavailable, enter the public job details
manually in ResumeStride. Always review the employer, source, and description before
saving. Long descriptions may be truncated and are marked for review.

PRIVACY AND TRUST

The extension reads the chosen tab only after you click it. It keeps one temporary
session capture for review or retry, expires it after 30 minutes of inactivity, and
sends it only to `https://resumestride.com` after you choose Send. It does not collect
browsing history across sites, read passwords or resume drafts, apply to jobs, run
background job searches, or receive your ResumeStride sign-in token.

A ResumeStride account is required to save a job. Free and Pro limits apply in the
web app. Match is not an ATS success score, hiring probability, or employment
guarantee.

CONTINUE IN RESUMESTRIDE

After reviewing the capture, continue in the ResumeStride web app to save the role,
see Match evidence, create a separate job-specific resume, and review optional
tailoring suggestions.

SUPPORT

Questions or capture trouble? Email `support@resumestride.com`. Include the public
job URL and what went wrong, but do not email a resume, password, or other sensitive
information.

## KEYWORDS / SEARCH INTENTS

Use these naturally in sentences; there is no keyword list to paste into the listing.

- **Primary:** save job; resume job match; compare resume to job; job description and
  resume; tailor resume to job
- **Secondary:** job-specific resume; resume comparison; resume tailoring; job match
  extension; job application assistant
- **Brand/precision:** evidence-based Match; master resume stays unchanged; review AI
  suggestions
- **Avoid as positioning:** AI resume optimizer; ATS score/checker; interview chance;
  beat the ATS; auto-apply; works everywhere; all job boards

Current competitors commonly lead with AI, percentage match scores, missing keywords,
ATS visibility, automatic rewriting, auto-apply, and broad compatibility. Several make
generic or weak claims such as “any page,” “works everywhere,” “exactly why,” or imply
that a score predicts qualification or callbacks. The consistent user expectation is a
fast job-page capture, visible strengths/gaps, specific next steps, privacy clarity, and
little or no copy/paste. ResumeStride should meet those expectations while owning the
more credible distinction: evidence, preserved uncertainty, separate versions, and
explicit user review.

Listings reviewed included Resume Match, Resume Tailor, Resume Copilot, FitToHire,
JobMatch, JobMatch AI, AI Resume Assistant, ResumeQuick, Teal, and lightweight job
trackers. Their most effective structure is: direct one-line outcome; “how it works”;
short feature bullets; site/support limits; privacy; setup or account requirements.
