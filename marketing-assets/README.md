# ResumeStride growth asset kit

Prepared locally for Growth Task 5. Nothing in this directory is published, deployed, or imported by the runtime application.

## Contents

- `demos/` — exact short-demo and micro-loop shot lists plus the reproducible still-capture runner.
- `stills/` — real-product hero frames in 16:9, 1:1, and 9:16, plus generation verification.
- `store/` — Chrome Web Store boundary and promotional-caption guidance; no extension package changes.
- `social/` — delivery and alt-text notes.
- `copy/` — draft X, LinkedIn, Product Hunt, Indie Hackers, Hacker News, and Chrome Web Store copy.
- `campaigns/` — canonical campaign URLs and the product-demo measurement plan.

## Safety and claims

Every capture uses the fictional Alex Morgan and Example Learning Co. fixture. No production account, customer resume, private job search, secret, testimonial, user count, or traction metric is included. Match statuses are evidence labels, not ATS scores or hiring predictions. AI suggestions remain user-reviewed and apply only to a separate job-specific version. Templates change presentation only.

Raw video and source recordings belong under `marketing-assets/demos/raw/`, which is ignored by Git. Reviewed delivery exports may be placed under `marketing-assets/demos/exports/` after codec, dimensions, playback, privacy, and claim checks. This task does not claim completed video files because a verified MP4 encoder is not available in the environment.
