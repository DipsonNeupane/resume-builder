## Indie Hackers-style launch post

**I built a resume workflow that keeps the master untouched**

Most tailoring flows become hard to trust when the original, the job evidence, and the generated wording blur together. ResumeStride keeps those boundaries visible: one master resume, saved jobs with requirement-level evidence, and one separate resume per opportunity.

The AI step is intentionally review-first. Each suggestion shows the current wording, proposed wording, and why it relates to the saved role. The user accepts, rejects, or edits it. There is no ATS percentage or claim that the product predicts hiring.

The demo uses fictional data and the real Patina application: https://resumestride.com/?utm_source=indie_hackers&utm_medium=community&utm_campaign=product-demo-launch&utm_content=founder-launch

I’m especially interested in whether the master → evidence → separate version flow is understandable without narration.
